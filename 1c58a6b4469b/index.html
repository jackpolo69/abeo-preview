section 01

What Abeó is

A family with an aging parent currently assembles their own care system: one company for caregivers, another for rides to dialysis, a third to find a facility when home stops working, and nobody holding the whole picture. Abeó is the coordination layer — one relationship, one record, one number to call.

Three services, one operator. In-home care from companions through CNAs. Medical transport, including wheelchair-accessible. Senior placement when a facility becomes the right answer. Because the same organisation carries all three, the transitions between them stop being where families fall through.

section 03

Why now

460,000+ adults over 65 in Orange County. Non-emergency medical transport alone is a US market moving from $6.6B in 2023 to a projected $13.43B by 2031 — about 9.3% compounding. The county reference rate for in-home care is $36–$48 an hour, which is where Abeó's card sits.

The consolidation pattern in adjacent care is already established: Optum at $5B, One Medical to Amazon at $3.9B, Kindred to Humana at $4.1B. Coordinated care with its own delivery capability is what those buyers have been acquiring.

Five ways in — pick any, pick several

◆ 1 · Solve your escort problem

The one closest to your own practice. Sedation means your patients cannot drive home, and a patient who arrives without a ride loses the slot — a cancelled procedure, a wasted prep, an afternoon of rescheduling. Abeó runs ambulatory transport at $35 base plus $2.50 a mile and wheelchair-accessible at $75 plus $4. Booked ahead against the procedure schedule, that cancellation category simply goes away. This is not a favour to us; it is a line item you are currently losing.

◆ 2 · Refer the ones who need more than a ride

Chronic GI patients — liver disease, IBD, feeding tubes, post-surgical recovery — frequently need support at home that nobody is coordinating. Same for the older patients who arrive alone and clearly should not be. No paperwork, no commitment, and it starts on Monday.

◆ 3 · Advise

A clinical voice on the advisory side. Service design, care standards, what actually goes wrong in the handoffs, and how a coordination layer should behave to be worth a physician's trust. Time only — an hour a month is real.

◆ 4 · Open a door

Facility and practice relationships are the growth engine here. A single 232-bed partner reshapes the referral picture — and so does a surgery centre or a GI group with the same escort problem, which is a room you are already standing in. An introduction from a physician carries weight a sales call never will.

◆ 5 · Invest

Abeó is raising a $1.5M–$2.5M seed at a $10M–$15M post-money valuation. Capital goes to caregiver recruitment and training, fleet, and the coordination platform that makes the density work. Full financials, the company evaluation and the model are available on request.

These are not a ladder and nobody is being moved up one. A physician who trusts the service enough to send patients is worth more to this company right now than a modest cheque from one who has not seen it work.
